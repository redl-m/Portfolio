About Me

Tennis 🎾

I've enjoyed playing tennis ever since I was a child and love playing against my friends or other tennis clubs in tennis leagues.

Causes 🌍

My greatest causes are sustainability and human equality regarding skin tone, background, gender and sexual orientation.

Travelling 🚆

Getting in touch with other cultures and nature is my biggest goal and I love everything about it. Travelling with friends and family is when I'm the happiest. Combining two interests – travelling and sustainability – lead to the goal of reaching as many places as possible by train. I savor the calm atmosphere of train journeys and enjoy organizing trips. This combination led to adventures to Paris, Brussels, Amsterdam, London, Edinburgh, Glasgow, Hamburg, Berlin, Copenhagen, Lund, Ystad, Stockholm, Venice and Milan.

Feel free to check out some of my favourite travel memories on the interactive map. :)
